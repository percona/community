Transparent Data Encryption (TDE) has long been a highly requested feature in the PostgreSQL ecosystem. The pg_tde project aims to fill this gap by providing encryption at rest with minimal impact on existing workflows and applications. In this lightning talk, I’ll present the current status of pg_tde, including recent progress, architecture highlights and key decisions.

We’ll go over what’s already working, what challenges remain and how to get involved. Whether you’re interested in security, compliance or PostgreSQL internals, this update will give you a quick but meaningful look at where PostgreSQL is with TDE and how it’s evolving.
