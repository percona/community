Vector search is moving from specialized systems into mainstream databases such as PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, and Elasticsearch.

This talk explains how embeddings are created, stored, and indexed; when to combine vector and traditional search; and how to measure speed and result quality. Attendees will gain practical guidance for choosing an architecture and building effective AI-powered search applications.
