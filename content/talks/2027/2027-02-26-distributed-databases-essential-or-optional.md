Distributed databases promise horizontal scale, but they also introduce operational complexity, consistency trade-offs, and cost. Can replicated single-node systems such as MySQL or PostgreSQL handle most workloads?

This talk compares distributed and non-distributed architectures, explains their practical limits, and gives developers a framework for deciding when distribution is essential—and when it is unnecessary.
