Everyone claims their AI model is "open source," but what does that actually mean when the weights, training data, and algorithms are hidden behind corporate walls?

A deep dive into the battle over the definition of Open Source AI (referencing the Open Source Initiative's framework). We will discuss why standardizing this definition is critical to preventing corporate capture of the next generation of technology.
