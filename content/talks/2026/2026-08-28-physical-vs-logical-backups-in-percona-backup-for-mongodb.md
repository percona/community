The talk provides a practical overview of physical and logical backup, with a technical deep dive into the differences between the two, and a comparison of backup/restore speed, backup size, ops complexity and functional limitations.

While the content targets users with existing product experience, new users benefit from a live demo and a preview of the backup use cases.

Key takeaways include:

Understanding backup execution within a MongoDB sharded cluster and the distributed agent-based architecture.

Previewing and comparing physical and logical backup storage layout.

Explaining and comparing technical benefits and trade-offs when executing logical/physical backups and restores.
