As the cloud evolves, so does the pressure to bring databases into Kubernetes, whether to maintain consistency with other applications or to facilitate the creation of an environment from scratch. But is this journey as simple as it seems?

In this talk, we will explore in practice the use of a Kubernetes operator for MySQL databases (Pecona Operators), covering implementation, essential operations such as backup, restore, high availability (HA), disaster recovery (DR), and troubleshooting, and how to migrate an environment to Kubernetes.
