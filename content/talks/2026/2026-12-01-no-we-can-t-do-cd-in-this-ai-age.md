Keynote at DevOpsCon Munich. Continuous delivery assumed stable pipelines, predictable releases, and humans in the loop for the hard calls. AI-assisted coding and agentic workflows change the blast radius of every commit — and revive old reasons teams still cannot ship continuously. A look at what still blocks CD when the toolchain starts writing (and changing) the software itself.
