DuckDB is showing up everywhere MySQL people look for analytics. This talk is for old-timers: what DuckDB actually is, how it differs from the InnoDB-shaped mental model, and what to expect when someone embeds a “duck” next to your OLTP data — without pretending it is just another storage engine.
