Can AI help us better understand our systems, or will it just give us another black box?

I'll be speaking at **Open Source Observability Day** with this talk. Let's look beyond the hype: where can AI genuinely improve observability, and how do we keep transparency and control over the systems we depend on? Open source deserves to be at the heart of that conversation.
