Running MySQL, PostgreSQL, and MongoDB on a single Kubernetes-based DBaaS platform is achievable — but operator patterns, multi-tenancy, and lifecycle management behave differently across engines. This talk cuts through the complexity with battle-tested architecture patterns, real trade-offs, and production lessons for building a unified, vendor-free DBaaS.
