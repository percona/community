A database operator can report a cluster as Ready long before anyone can actually use it. Credentials, network access, backups, monitoring, and sane defaults still have to land before an application team gets a working database.

This lightning talk looks at that gap in a self-service DBaaS built on Kubernetes operators: what Ready means at the operator level, what users expect it to mean, and what the platform has to own to close the difference.

Lightning talk at the CNCF Platform Engineering booth (P-16B), Solutions Showcase, KubeCon + CloudNativeCon North America 2026.
