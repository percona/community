Everyone is talking about LLMs and GenAI, but no one is talking about the massive data infrastructure strain behind them. If your database bottlenecks, your AI is useless.

The hidden infrastructure costs of the AI boom. How multi-model and highly optimized open-source databases (Postgres, MySQL) are adapting to handle vector workloads alongside traditional data, and how to scale without breaking the bank.
