Achieving observability in infrastructure is crucial but traditionally resource-intensive, requiring extensive application instrumentation. This talk explores a transformative approach using eBPF to efficiently gather telemetry data - metrics, logs, and traces - without the overhead of conventional methods. We will discuss how eBPF tracks container activities such as network calls and filesystem operations, providing insights with minimal impact on resources. The session will also cover the effective use of this data for troubleshooting within Kubernetes environments. Learn how eBPF offers a streamlined, powerful tool for enhancing observability, simplifying operations, and improving system performance. Join us to discover how to leverage eBPF for better visibility and control over your Kubernetes clusters.
