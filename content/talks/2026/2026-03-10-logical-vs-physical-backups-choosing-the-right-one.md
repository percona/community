We'll start by defining what logical backups are and explore when they shine—like selective restores, version upgrades, and lightweight migrations. Then we'll look at the flip side: where logical backups fail under scale, performance pressure, and complex workloads. Next, we'll break down physical backups: what they are, why they're the gold standard for full cluster recovery, and the scenarios where they deliver unbeatable speed and reliability. We'll also highlight the practical challenges and risks that come with physical backups. Finally, we'll compare both approaches side-by-side and close with clear, actionable guidance on how to choose the best strategy—or combination—for your environment.
