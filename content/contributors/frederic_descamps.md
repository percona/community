@lefred has been consulting Open Source and MySQL for almost 20 years. After graduating in Management Information Technology, Frédéric Descamps started his career as a developer for an ERP under HPUX. He then moved into open source, joining one of the first Belgian startups dedicated to free software around GNU/Linux. In 2011 he joined Percona; in 2016 he moved to the MySQL Community Team as MySQL Community Manager for EMEA & APAC. He is now with the MariaDB Foundation. Frédéric is a regular speaker at open source conferences. His blog is mostly about MySQL: https://lefred.be.
