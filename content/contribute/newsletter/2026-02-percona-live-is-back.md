## Percona Live Returns!

<a href="https://perconalive.com/"><img src="/newsletter/2026-02/percona-live.png" alt="Percona Live 2026, Bay Area" width="1120" height="267"></a>

Computer History Museum (Mountain View, California). May 27–29, 2026.

Percona Live represents Percona’s flagship industry event and most visible platform. Join us in Mountain View, California at the Computer History Museum for an event focused on engaging customers, prospects, partners, and the open-source database community.

Whether you are interested in [being one of our exclusive sponsors](https://perconalive.com/2026-usa/sponsoring) of the event, [participating as a speaker](https://perconalive.com/2026-usa/cfp) through our call for papers and speaker submission process or just excited about [saving your spot for Percona Live](https://perconalive.com/), you’ll be sure to find the return of Percona’s premier in-person and thought-leadership event worth your while!
