## Percona + Amsterdam = Kubecon Europe!

<img src="/newsletter/2026-04-kubecon-highlights/WhatsApp-Image-2026-03-27-at-09.29.37-4-.jpeg" alt="Percona booth at KubeCon Europe 2026" width="800" height="600">

<img src="/newsletter/2026-04-kubecon-highlights/WhatsApp-Image-2026-03-27-at-09.30.51-3-.jpeg" alt="Conversation at the Percona booth during KubeCon Europe" width="520" height="390">

<img src="/newsletter/2026-04-kubecon-highlights/IMG_5550.jpg" alt="Percona team at KubeCon Europe 2026" width="520" height="390">

KubeCon Europe 2026 in Amsterdam was full of highlights! Percona was a proud Silver sponsor, and we loved our chance to be around so many friends and members of our open source community!

Percona Founder Peter Zaitsev delivered wise words on applying AI to query optimization and Principal Field Technologist, Michal Nosek, delivered a talk at our partner Chainguard’s booth, and Tech Evangelist Edith Puclla hosted Data on Kubernetes Day where experts shared insights on real-world data challenges in Kubernetes.

Raffle prizes, swag, networking, discussions, demos, and laughs were abundant at our booth and that’s thanks to all of you in the open source community!
