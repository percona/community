## The Countdown Is On—Percona Live Is Only 4 Weeks Away

It’s time—Percona Live is almost here, just mere weeks away on May 27–29! The excitement is building as we prepare to bring together an incredible community, made possible in large part by our amazing sponsors—we truly couldn’t do it without their support and commitment.
