## Percona Mountaineers Keep on Climbing!

The Percona community recognizes your community contributions and offers rewards as you climb from Basecamp to the summit.

How it works. Everyone starts at Basecamp. From there, you climb based on how you engage with the community:

- GitHub — issues, PRs, and merged contributions
- Forum — starting discussions, replying, and accepted solutions
- Content — blog posts and tutorials, including through our [Community Writers Program](/blog/2026/05/22/write-for-percona-community/)
- Direct feedback — 1:1 sessions with our engineering team, telling us how you’re using our tools and what you’d like to see next

Remain active in more than one of these in the same month, and your points multiply. There’s no single right way to climb — code, conversation, and content all count.
