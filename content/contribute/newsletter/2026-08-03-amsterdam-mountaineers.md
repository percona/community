## Percona Mountaineers

Points start at Basecamp and come from four places:

- GitHub: issues, pull requests, and merged contributions
- Forum: discussions, replies, and accepted solutions
- Content: blog posts and tutorials, including the [Community Writers Program](/blog/2026/05/22/write-for-percona-community/)
- Direct feedback: 1:1 sessions with the engineering team

Activity in more than one of those in the same month multiplies the points.

[Become a Mountaineer](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeH_ube0G4TLMP_2Kh5oa1OSSNEaUExiIp1E6qzfUzg9dDD9Q/viewform).
