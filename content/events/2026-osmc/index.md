**OSMC 2026** is the **Open Source Monitoring Conference** — twentieth anniversary edition. Three days in **Nuremberg** for people who build and run monitoring and observability stacks: workshops, talks, and plenty of hallway time with the same community that ships the tools.

- 📅 **November 17–19, 2026** — workshops on the 17th; conference talks on the 18th–19th
- 📍 **Nuremberg, Germany**
- 🏛 **Venue:** Scandic Nürnberg Central (Frauentorgraben 11)
- 🔗 **Official website:** [https://osmc.de/](https://osmc.de/)

OSMC stays focused on **open source** monitoring and observability — comparing approaches, sharing production war stories, and meeting the maintainers and operators who work on this day to day. Sessions are in English; talks are recorded for attendees.

Percona is there with talks on open source monitoring history and vector-search observability. If you are at OSMC, come say hello and compare notes.
