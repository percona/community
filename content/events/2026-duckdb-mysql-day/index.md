**DuckDB × MySQL Day 2026** is a one-day **online** conference on **November 17, 2026**, hosted by the [OurSQL Foundation](https://oursqlfoundation.org/). Theme: fast analytics next to MySQL and MariaDB — columnar engines, analytical replication, Parquet, and whether HTAP is finally practical.

- 📅 **November 17, 2026**
- 🌐 **Online**
- 🔗 **Schedule and registration:** [oursqlfoundation.org/events/duckdb-mysql-day](https://oursqlfoundation.org/events/duckdb-mysql-day/)

## Talks

### DuckDB for MySQL Old Timers

**Speaker:** [Peter Zaitsev](/contributors/peter_zaitsev/) (Percona)

DuckDB through the eyes of someone who has lived in MySQL for a long time — what maps cleanly, what does not, and where the “duck in your OLTP database” pitch gets interesting.

[Talk page](/talks/2026/2026-11-17-duckdb-for-mysql-old-timers/)

### Duck Hunt: An Independent Benchmark of DuckDB-Powered MySQL

**Speaker:** [Evgeniy Patlan](/contributors/evgeniy_patlan/) (Percona)

Same TPC-H workload, same hardware, several engines that bolted a duck onto MySQL-class systems — plus InnoDB, PostgreSQL, and plain DuckDB. Speeds are real; surprises too.

[Talk page](/talks/2026/2026-11-17-duck-hunt-an-independent-benchmark-of-duckdb-powered-mysql/)

### Twenty Years of Bolting Analytics onto MySQL: Will DuckDB Finally Make It HTAP?

**Speaker:** [Vadim Tkachenko](/contributors/vadim_tkachenko/) (OurSQL Foundation)

Vadim speaks here as president of the OurSQL Foundation (not as a Percona product talk). Two decades of bolting analytics onto MySQL — and whether DuckDB changes the HTAP story.

[Talk page](/talks/2026/2026-11-17-twenty-years-of-bolting-analytics-onto-mysql-will-duckdb-finally-make-it-htap/)
