**PGConf.PL 2026**, the first PostgreSQL community conference in Poland, took place on **November 24** at **ARCHE Dwór Uphagena** in Gdańsk. The one-day program, organized by PostgreSQL Europe, was picked from 123 proposals by 57 speakers and covered AI and SQL, VACUUM and asynchronous I/O in PostgreSQL 18, huge pages, Patroni, and multi-tenant design.

- 📅 **November 24, 2026**
- 📍 **ARCHE Dwór Uphagena, Gdańsk**
- 🔗 **Event page:** [2026.pgconf.pl](https://2026.pgconf.pl/) · [Schedule](https://www.postgresql.eu/events/pgconfpl2026/schedule/)

## Percona at PGConf.PL

Percona sponsored the first edition.

[Jan Wieremjewicz](/contributors/jan_wieremjewicz/) is on the PGConf.PL organizing committee. He opened and closed the day together with Andreas Scherbaum and hosted the silver sponsor lightning talks.

![Opening of PGConf.PL 2026 in Gdańsk](/events/2026-pgconf-pl/pgconf-pl-2026-opening.jpg)

### [Migrate from Oracle to PostgreSQL they said…](/talks/2026/2026-11-24-migrate-from-oracle-to-postgresql-they-said)

**Speaker:** [Michal Nosek](/contributors/michal_nosek/) · Lightning talk, 13:55–14:40 block, Sala u Profesora B

Michal's lightning talk was about migrating from Oracle to PostgreSQL. Percona and Guardioz each had a slot in the block.

[Session page](https://www.postgresql.eu/events/pgconfpl2026/schedule/session/8677-silver-sponsor-lightning-talks/)

Thanks to the PGConf.PL organizers and PostgreSQL Europe for starting a PostgreSQL conference in Poland.
