### Physical vs Logical Backups in PostgreSQL

**Speaker:** [Sonia Valeja](/contributors/sonia_valeja/)

Sonia compares physical and logical backups in PostgreSQL: what each captures and how that changes a restore. Logical backups give selective restores and help with migrations and test environments. Physical backups cover full-cluster recovery, WAL, and point-in-time recovery. The session also compares restore speed, database size, version compatibility, and disaster recovery, and argues that recovery requirements should drive the backup choice.
