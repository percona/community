Percona is back as a **Bronze sponsor** at **AWS re:Invent**, November 30-December 4 in **Las Vegas**. Find us at **booth #1486** on the Expo floor.

- 📅 **November 30-December 4, 2026**
- 📍 **Las Vegas, Nevada**
- 🏛 **Venue:** Venetian Convention and Expo Center
- 🧭 **Percona booth:** #1486, Expo
- 🔗 **Official website:** https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/

**AWS re:Invent** is one of the largest cloud conferences of the year: tens of thousands of builders, engineers, and operators, a week of keynotes, deep technical sessions, and hands-on labs across the AWS ecosystem. The Expo runs at the Venetian Convention and Expo Center, and that's where our team will be.

Stop by **booth #1486** to talk with Percona engineers about running **MySQL**, **PostgreSQL**, and **MongoDB** on AWS: performance tuning, replication and high availability, backups, and where the cost actually goes in production. Bring the problem you're dealing with right now; that's usually the best conversation.

Want more time than a booth chat allows? Reach out ahead of time and we'll set up a meeting with Perconians on site during the week.
