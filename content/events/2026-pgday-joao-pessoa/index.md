**PGDay João Pessoa 2026**, the second edition of the PostgreSQL community day in Paraíba, Brazil, took place on **November 26** at **Centro Universitário UNIESP** in Cabedelo, next to João Pessoa. Entry was free, and the program covered open source, AI, automation, database administration, and performance. The event was supported by Associação PostgreSQL Brasil and UNIESP.

## Percona talk

### [Diagnóstico de Performance com pg_gather](/talks/2026/2026-11-26-diagnostico-de-performance-com-pg-gather)

**Speaker:** [Breno Baima](/contributors/breno_baima/)

Breno, a Database Engineer at Percona and one of the PGDay João Pessoa organizers, showed how to diagnose PostgreSQL performance with pg_gather. He walked through the three steps: collecting data with `gather.sql`, importing it with `gather_schema.sql`, and generating the report with `gather_report.sql`. The talk was in Portuguese.

[Talk page](/talks/2026/2026-11-26-diagnostico-de-performance-com-pg-gather)

The other speakers were Joao Foltran, William Lino Oliveira, Luan Silva, Francisco Porfírio Ribeiro Neto, and Euler Taveira. Thanks to the organizers and to everyone who came.
