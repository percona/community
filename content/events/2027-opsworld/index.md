## Percona talk

### eBPF for AI-Powered Root Cause Analysis

**Speaker:** [Peter Zaitsev](/contributors/peter_zaitsev/)

More telemetry does not fix incidents faster by itself. Peter looks at how eBPF gives deep, low-overhead visibility into running systems, and how to pair that signal with AI techniques to get from “something’s wrong” to a diagnosis without drowning in dashboards.
