**ConFoo Montreal 2027** takes place on **February 24–26** at the **Bonaventure Hotel** in Montreal. ConFoo is a three-day developer conference with 186 sessions across tracks like AI, architecture, DevOps, security, and databases.

- 📅 **February 24–26, 2027**
- 📍 **Bonaventure Hotel, Montreal**
- 🔗 **Event page:** [confoo.ca/en/2027](https://www.confoo.ca/en/2027)

## Percona talks

Both talks are on Friday, February 26, in room ST-Laurent 8.

### Distributed Databases: Essential or Optional?

**Speaker:** [Peter Zaitsev](/contributors/peter_zaitsev/) · Friday, February 26, 09:00, ST-Laurent 8

Distributed databases scale out, but you pay for it in operational complexity, consistency trade-offs, and cost. Peter compares distributed and single-node architectures, shows where replicated MySQL and PostgreSQL stop being enough, and gives you a way to decide when distribution is worth it.

[Talk page](/talks/2027/2027-02-26-distributed-databases-essential-or-optional/) · [Session page](https://confoo.ca/en/2027/session/distributed-databases-essential-or-optional)

### Vector Search in Modern Databases

**Speaker:** [Peter Zaitsev](/contributors/peter_zaitsev/) · Friday, February 26, 15:00, ST-Laurent 8

Vector search no longer needs a separate system: PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, and Elasticsearch all support it. Peter covers how embeddings are created, stored, and indexed, when to combine vector and keyword search, and how to measure both speed and result quality.

[Talk page](/talks/2027/2027-02-26-vector-search-in-modern-databases/) · [Session page](https://confoo.ca/en/2027/session/vector-search-in-modern-databases-1)

If you're at ConFoo, catch Peter after either session with your database questions.
