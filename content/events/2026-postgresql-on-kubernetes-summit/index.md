**PostgreSQL on Kubernetes Summit @ PGConf.EU 2026** is a Community Events Day session — afternoon talks and panels on running PostgreSQL in cloud-native environments. Operators, backups, observability, and production lessons, with maintainers and practitioners in the same room.

- 📅 **October 23, 2026** · 13:30–16:45
- 📍 **Valencia, Spain** · Room **Audit 2** (Palacio de Congresos)
- 🔗 **Event page:** [PostgreSQL on Kubernetes Summit](https://2026.pgconf.eu/community-events/pg_on_kubernetes/)
- 🏛 Hosted during [PGConf Europe 2026](/events/2026-pgconf-europe/)

Organised by Gülçin Yıldırım Jelinek and Floor Drees. Format: short talks plus panel Q&A.

Percona engineers speaking:

- **Chetan Shivashankar** — [Swap or Stop? How a Little Swap Can Keep PostgreSQL Running](/talks/2026/2026-10-23-swap-or-stop-how-a-little-swap-can-keep-postgresql-running/)
- **George Kechagias** — [The Anatomy of a PostgreSQL Backup CRD: pgBackRest, Multi-Repo, and Tiered Storage](/talks/2026/2026-10-23-the-anatomy-of-a-postgresql-backup-crd-pgbackrest-multi-repo-and-tiered-storage/)
