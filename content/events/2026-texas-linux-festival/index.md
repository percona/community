## Percona talk

### Using eBPF for Kubernetes Observability Without the Overhead

**Speaker:** [Peter Zaitsev](/contributors/peter_zaitsev/)

Getting metrics, logs, and traces out of infrastructure usually means instrumenting everything and paying for it in CPU and complexity. Peter walks through how eBPF can collect that telemetry from container activity — network calls, filesystem operations, and related signals — with much less overhead, and how to use it when you are troubleshooting Kubernetes in the real world.

[Talk page](/talks/2026/2026-11-06-using-ebpf-for-kubernetes-observability-without-the-overhead/) · [Conference site](https://2026.texaslinuxfest.org/)

If you are in Austin for TXLF, find Peter between sessions. Bring the observability problem you are stuck on — that conversation is usually more useful than the slide deck.
