## Percona talk

### Your platform team has no story, and that's why you have no budget

**Speaker:** [Valeria Bogatyreva](/contributors/valeria_bogatyreva/) · Monday, November 30, 09:15 GST

A platform team walks into the annual budget meeting with a list of work it finished. A vendor walks in with a story about what the business gets. Valeria treats the platform team as a product team: internal developers are the users, the CFO or CTO is the buyer, and the budget cycle is the renewal. The talk turns four go-to-market frameworks into artifacts the team can build the following Monday.
